About

I am a medical AI deep learning researcher. My research focuses on point cloud generation, trustworthy AI, and 3D segmentation.

I am extremely self-motivated and committed to translating deep learning algorithms into real clinical settings.

I started my research in September 2024 as an undergraduate junior, and have worked in this field for roughly two years. I independently contacted a professor to join their lab. Without assigned projects, I shaped my own research and collaborated with a senior labmate. I now lead a research group I founded to investigate trustworthy AI, using clinical dental data as our testbed.

Research Interests

  • 🛡️ Trustworthy AI — uncertainty and evidence-driven modeling for medical imaging and clinical diagnosis in dentistry
  • 📷 3D Medical Vision — segmentation, point cloud generation, shape completion and anatomical modeling on IOS/CBCT
  • 🤖 Foundation Models & Vision-Language Agents — cross-domain adaptation under limited labels for automated multi-task medical AI

🏢 Dental-Gradient Research Group

Founder & Lead Researcher

Dental-Gradient is a cross-university research group with over 15 members, founded to close the gap between stomatology clinical practice and artificial intelligence. The group integrates clinical interns, dental clinicians and deep learning developers to build open, high-quality oral AI datasets and algorithms.

Research Details：

  • Launched the orthodontic multi-modal dataset project: curating 1100+ cases combining IOS intraoral scans and CBCT volumetric data for model training and benchmarking.
  • Design dataset annotation pipelines, anatomical label specification, quality control, and ethical review protocols for clinical imaging data.
  • Coordinate cross-school collaboration, manage research milestones, code review, and manuscript writing for group projects.
  • Build open-source code releases, visual demos and result repositories while strictly protecting raw clinical patient data under ethical constraints.

Long-term goal: Construct a trusted foundational model for oral 3D vision, supporting orthodontics, restoration and oral lesion auxiliary diagnosis.
